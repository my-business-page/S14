A few useful answers before your appointment. For anything specific, just message us.

How do I book?

WhatsApp is the primary booking and enquiry channel. Send your preferred service and date, and confirm availability directly.

Which treatment should I choose?

If you are unsure, describe the look you want and your current brow/lash situation. The team can guide you toward the appropriate available service.

Can I ask for the latest prices?

Yes. Menu items and prices can change, so message WhatsApp for the current service menu before booking.

Can I see recent work?

Visit the studio's Instagram for current beauty work and updates.
